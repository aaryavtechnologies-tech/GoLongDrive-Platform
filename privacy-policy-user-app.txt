2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
================================================================================
To deliver a secure and seamless taxi booking experience, Go Long Drive collects the following categories of information:

A. Personal & Account Information
- Full Name
- Mobile Phone Number (used for account identification, SMS communications, and driver coordination)
- Email Address (for account authentication, booking confirmations, tax invoices, and service notices)
- Account Password (securely encrypted using salted bcrypt hashing on our backend)
- Optional Profile Details: Gender, Date of Birth, Emergency Contact Name, and Emergency Contact Phone Number
- Unique 4-Digit Ride Confirmation PIN (generated by the platform for passenger authentication before trip start)

B. Precise and Approximate Location Information (Location Services)
- FOREGROUND LOCATION ONLY: When you open and actively use Go Long Drive, we collect precise location data (using GPS) and approximate location data (using network/cellular signals) via Android permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION).
- Purpose of Location Data:
  * To automatically detect your pickup location on the map and recommend nearby addresses.
  * To calculate accurate driving routes, distances, and estimated travel duration using Google Maps.
  * To calculate transparent, distance-based trip fares.
  * To match your booking request with available drivers in your vicinity.
- NO BACKGROUND LOCATION TRACKING:
  * Go Long Drive accesses location strictly in the foreground while the application is actively open on your screen.
  * Go Long Drive DOES NOT collect background location (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION) and does NOT run any background location tracking service when the application is closed or minimized.
  * During an active journey, live vehicle movement displayed on your map is streamed from our platform servers via real-time WebSockets, not tracked from your device's background location.
  * We never sell or share location data with third-party advertisers.

C. Booking, Travel & Tour Details
- Pickup and drop-off addresses and geographic coordinates.
- Selected vehicle category (e.g., Sedan, SUV, Premium).
- Tour package bookings, destination itineraries, travel dates, and package preferences.
- Ride history, timestamps, ride statuses (Searching, Assigned, Started, Completed, Cancelled).
- Digital Boarding Pass data and trip summaries.

D. Financial & Transaction Data
- Payment Method Chosen: Online digital payments (Cards, UPI, NetBanking, Wallets).
- Transaction History: Final trip fare, advance amounts, Razorpay payment order IDs, payment date and timestamp, payment status (Paid, Pending).
- Third-Party Payment Gateway: All digital payments are processed through Razorpay Software Private Limited (an RBI-authorized and PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateway). Go Long Drive DOES NOT collect, process, or store sensitive credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or bank netbanking passwords on our servers.

E. Device & Technical Information
- Device manufacturer, model, operating system version, and IP address.
- Network connection status and basic application performance logs to maintain app stability and optimize performance.

F. Customer Support & Communications
- Support ticket submissions, inquiry messages, feedback notes, and customer rating submissions sent to our helpdesk.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
================================================================================
We utilize the collected information strictly for legitimate business and operational purposes:
1. Facilitating Transportation Services: Processing ride requests, matching passengers with nearby drivers, verifying rides using the 4-digit Ride PIN, and displaying live journey progress.
2. Fare Computation: Accurately calculating trip fares based on distance traveled, duration, and vehicle rates.
3. Communications: Sending transactional SMS, real-time socket alerts for driver arrival, trip updates, digital boarding passes, and receipts.
4. Security & Fraud Prevention: Verifying customer identities, protecting against fraudulent transactions, and ensuring passenger safety.
5. Tour Packages & Itineraries: Organizing and managing curated tour packages, sightseeing schedules, and customer inquiries.
6. Customer Support: Investigating and resolving ride disputes, payment inquiries, or support tickets.
7. Legal & Regulatory Compliance: Complying with applicable transport regulations, tax laws, and lawful government requests.

4. SHARING AND DISCLOSURE OF INFORMATION
================================================================================
We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party data brokers or marketing agencies. We share information only under the following limited circumstances:

A. With Assigned Drivers
When a booking is confirmed, we share relevant details with the assigned driver solely to complete the trip:
- Passenger's first name
- Pickup address and destination address
- Passenger phone number (to coordinate pickup)
- Passenger 4-digit Ride PIN (which the passenger reveals to the driver upon vehicle arrival to authorize trip start)

B. With Third-Party Service Providers (SDKs & Infrastructure)
We partner with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform under strict confidentiality and data protection agreements:
1. Google Maps Platform (Google LLC):
   - SDKs / APIs: google_maps_flutter, Google Places API, and Google Directions API.
   - Data Received: Geographic coordinates, address search queries, and device IP address.
   - Purpose: Geocoding, address autocomplete, driving route polyline calculation, and interactive map displays.
   - Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
2. Razorpay Software Private Limited:
   - SDK: razorpay_flutter.
   - Data Received: Transaction order ID, payment amount, customer contact email/phone, and payment confirmation status.
   - Purpose: Secure processing of digital payments (UPI, debit/credit cards, net banking).
   - Privacy Policy: https://razorpay.com/privacy/
3. Cloud Hosting & Database Infrastructure:
   - Secure encrypted cloud servers and MongoDB Atlas for database persistence.
- Note: Go Long Drive does NOT integrate Firebase (no Firebase Messaging, Crashlytics, or Analytics), Sentry, or any third-party advertising SDKs.

C. For Legal & Safety Reasons
We may disclose information if required by law, subpoena, court order, or governmental regulation, or when necessary to protect the safety, rights, or property of Go Long Drive, our users, or the general public.

5. DATA RETENTION AND SECURITY
================================================================================
- Data Security Measures: We implement industry-standard technical and organizational security measures, including 256-bit SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit, salted bcrypt password hashing on our backend servers, encrypted client token storage (flutter_secure_storage utilizing Android Keystore / EncryptedSharedPreferences), secure OTP verification for phone authentication, and role-based access restrictions to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
- Retention Period: We retain your account information, ride records, and invoices for as long as your account remains active, or as required by applicable tax, transportation, and accounting laws.

6. ACCOUNT & DATA DELETION POLICY (GOOGLE PLAY COMPLIANT)
================================================================================
Go Long Drive respects your right to control your personal data. You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account & Data Deletion:
1. Through the Mobile Application:
   - Open Go Long Drive User App -> Navigate to Profile -> Tap "Delete Account" -> Confirm your request in the dialog. The deletion request is automatically registered with our systems and your session will be safely logged out.
2. Online Public Web Deletion Portal:
   - Visit our designated web deletion page: https://golongdrive.online/account-deletion
   - Submit your registered Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
   - Our administrative team will verify and process your request within 7 business days.
3. Email Request:
   - You may send a deletion request from your registered email address to support@golongdrive.online with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Please include your full name and registered phone number.

What Happens Upon Account Deletion:
- Your profile details, email address, phone number, saved addresses, and active login tokens will be permanently erased from our active production databases within 30 days.
- Completed trip billing invoices and transactional audit records will be retained in anonymized, secure archives strictly as mandated by Indian taxation, financial, and transport regulations.

8. PERMISSIONS REQUESTED BY GO LONG DRIVE (ANDROID)
================================================================================
Go Long Drive requests ONLY the following permissions, exactly as declared in the Android application manifest:
1. ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION: Required to detect your precise GPS pickup location on the map, match nearby available cabs, and calculate driving routes.
2. ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Required for approximate network/cellular-based location detection.
3. INTERNET: Required to connect to our secure backend API, stream real-time trip status via WebSockets, and process digital payments.
4. ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to monitor network connectivity status and ensure seamless communication with platform services.

Phone Dialing Notice:
- Tapping "Call Driver" in the application opens your device's native phone dialer via standard system intents (tel: protocol); Go Long Drive DOES NOT request or require direct phone call permissions (CALL_PHONE).

No Unnecessary Permissions:
- Go Long Drive DOES NOT request or use background location (ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION), foreground services, camera permissions, photo/media storage permissions, or notification permissions.
